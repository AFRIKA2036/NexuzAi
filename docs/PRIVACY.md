# NexuzAI Privacy Policy
*Last Updated: May 28, 2026*

NexuzAI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our AI Agents application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information
- **Account Data:** When you sign up, we collect your email address and full name via Supabase Authentication.
- **Payment Data:** When you upgrade to Pro or Team plans, payment information is processed by Paystack. We do not store credit card details on our servers; we only store references and customer codes provided by the payment processor.

### 1.2 Usage Data
- **Prompts and Outputs:** We store the text prompts you provide to AI agents and the resulting AI-generated outputs to allow you to view your generation history.
- **Activity Logs:** We track the number of daily requests to enforce usage limits.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To provide and maintain our service.
- To manage your account and subscription.
- To improve our AI agents and user experience.
- To comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
- **Supabase:** Your account data and generation history are stored in Supabase, which employs industry-standard security measures.
- **Encryption:** All data in transit is encrypted using TLS.
- **Access Control:** We use Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure that only you can access your own data.

## 4. Third-Party Services
- **OpenRouter / AI Providers:** Your prompts are sent to third-party AI providers (e.g., OpenRouter, NVIDIA) to generate responses. We do not send your personal account information (like email) to these providers.
- **Paystack:** Used for payment processing.

## 5. Your Rights
Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Request the deletion of your data.
- Opt-out of certain data processing.
